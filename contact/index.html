Call the 24/7 line

The fastest way to reach us is by phone, and the line is answered 24/7. Call (760) 769-1159 and tell dispatch where the water is coming from, how far it has spread and whether the power is affected.

We do not use contact forms. A phone call gets a person who can tell you the next safe step right away, which matters more than a message that waits in an inbox.

Before you call

A few details make the call faster.

  • Where the water is coming from, if you know
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how far it has spread
  • Whether you have shut off the main water valve
  • Whether power to the wet area is on
  • Photos or video of the damage, if you can take them safely
