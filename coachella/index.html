The long heat season and what it does to plumbing

Summer in Coachella means months of triple-digit afternoons, and the AC runs almost constantly. Condensate lines clog, drain pans overflow, and water heaters in garages take thermal stress. Many calls start with a ceiling stain or a damp closet floor.

Hot ground also loosens fittings as pipes expand and contract. If a line fails at night while the house is closed up for cooling, you may find water by morning. Call (760) 769-1159 day or night, and ask about emergency water removal as soon as you see it.

The rare storm that still floods

This area gets very little rain in a normal year, but it can arrive all at once. During Tropical Storm Hilary in August 2023, the NWS two-day totals reported nearby were 2.35 inches at Thermal Airport and 2.29 inches at Kent Sea Tech in Mecca. That is a large share of an ordinary year of rain falling on dry, hard ground.

Runoff, not just roof leaks, entered garages and lower entries. Sandbags were available at the Coachella Senior Center on Seventh Street and the Corporate Yard on Enterprise Way, so check with the city when a forecast calls for rain. For rising water, the job is flood damage restoration, and flood is usually excluded by standard homeowners insurance.

Empty houses, seasonal work and unnoticed leaks

Farm work and travel mean many homes sit empty for days or weeks. A pinhole leak in a vacant house can soak flooring unseen, and an overflowing washing machine hose is a Category 2 gray water event once it stands. Leave your main water shutoff closed when you travel and ask a neighbor to check in.

If you come home to a wet floor at night or on a weekend, call us and keep everyone out of the standing water. We are open 24/7 and a dispatcher will walk through the first steps.

Irrigation season and outdoor water that finds the house

Irrigation season brings a different risk. Sprinkler heads aimed at stucco, broken drip lines and low grading can push water against slab edges and into walls. If a baseboard feels damp after watering days, the likely cause is outside.

Fixing the grade and the sprinkler is the homeowner's job, but drying the wall is ours. A crew uses water mitigation methods such as air movers and dehumidifiers until moisture meters read normal.

Which Coachella this is, and who serves it

The City of Coachella is a different place from the Coachella Valley and from the Coachella music festival, which is held in Indio at the Empire Polo Club. If you are in the city of Coachella, the Coachella Water Authority, managed by the City of Coachella, is likely your water provider. The Coachella Valley Water District also has an office here.

The lower Whitewater River becomes the Coachella Valley Stormwater Channel, which is a regional flood channel and not something to approach during storms. For a gas smell or downed line, call the utility or 911 before you call us.

If water is still spreading in Coachella

While you wait for a crew in Coachella, these five steps slow the damage.

Shut off at the source

Turn the fixture valve or the main clockwise. In summer, a bursting AC line or water heater can run all night, so treat it as an emergency.

Check the bill for the provider

The City of Coachella manages the Coachella Water Authority, and CVWD serves other nearby areas. Your bill names yours.

Cool down the room, not the panel

Open a window if it is safe, but leave electrical panels alone unless the floor beneath is dry.

Move people and pets out

Standing water from drains or outdoors is contaminated. Keep children and animals in dry rooms.

Photograph and bag small items

Take photos of water lines, then lift papers, shoes and cords. Leave heavy cleanup to the crew.
