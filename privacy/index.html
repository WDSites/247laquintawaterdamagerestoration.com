What this policy covers

This policy explains how 247 La Quinta Water Damage Restoration handles information when you visit this website or call our line. This site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout, so we collect very little.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call. We use call details such as the address, the type of damage and your contact number to arrange service and to follow up on your request.

Website data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed. We use it to keep the site secure and working. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the maps on our pages are Google Maps embeds, which may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Sharing and your choices

We share information only as needed to arrange and complete the work you requested, or when the law requires it. You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@247laquintawaterdamagerestoration.com. We may update this policy, and the current version always lives on this page.
